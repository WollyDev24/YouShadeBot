YouShade Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what data the YouShade bot and its web dashboard collect, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. The bot and dashboard are run by the server owner who installed them.

1. Data collected when you sign in to the dashboard with Discord

When you use Sign in with Discord, the bot asks Discord for permission to read your profile and your servers. This includes:

  • Your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar.
  • The list of servers you are a member of (used only to show the servers in the panel that you share with the bot).

The bot does not read your messages, email address, phone number, or anything else from your Discord account. Only the scopes identify and guilds are requested. Discord shows you exactly what is being requested before you approve it.

2. Data stored by the bot's features

The bot stores the settings you configure through the dashboard or Discord commands. Depending on which features you use, examples are:

  • Server and channel IDs for features such as temporary channels, ticket panels, stats and logs.
  • Contents you configure, such as welcome messages, sticky messages, auto-responses, announcements, giveaways and reminders.
  • Moderation records: automod cases, word filters, spam settings, lockdown records, warn/ban/mute actions.
  • Participation data: giveaway entries, poll votes, survey responses, counting stats, leveling XP and roles.

Discord user IDs and usernames may be recorded where a feature needs to attribute something (for example who entered a giveaway or answered a survey).

3. Where data is stored and who can see it

  • All data is stored locally on the machine that runs the bot, in the bot's own data directory.
  • The panel password is stored only as a one-way hash — the plaintext password can never be recovered from it.
  • Discord sign-in sessions keep a small session token in your browser as an httpOnly cookie. The server keeps the Discord access/refresh tokens needed to keep you signed in in a local sessions file.
  • Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or sent to any third party. Discord OAuth tokens are never logged.
  • There are no analytics or tracking pixels of any kind.

4. Who can access the dashboard and what can they do

When Discord OAuth is not configured, signing in with the panel password makes you a full administrator. When Discord OAuth is configured, the password sign-in is disabled and only Discord sign-in is available: you can view and change settings only on servers you own or have the Administrator permission on. The panel owner may grant full administrator rights to specific Discord accounts (via the PANEL_ADMIN_USERS setting); those accounts can manage every server they are in. Nobody else can see or edit a server's settings unless they are signed in and satisfy the same checks.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Feature data (welcome messages, filters, etc.) is kept until you disable or delete the feature, or until the bot is removed from your server.
  • Discord sign-in sessions last up to 30 days or until you press "Log out" — whichever comes first.
  • To stop the bot collecting any more data, remove it from your server or uninstall it. To delete stored data, ask the server owner to wipe the bot's data directory or disable the relevant features.

Because an instance of this bot is self-hosted, the server owner ultimately controls its data. If you have questions about a specific deployment, contact the person who runs that instance.

6. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if features change the data that is collected. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
